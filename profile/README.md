Keiyu is the verified knowledge layer for the daily operation of small and mid-sized companies in Latin America. It connects the email, WhatsApp conversations, documents and systems a company already uses, answers with the exact sentence from the source, warns about what is due, what does not add up and what is missing, and helps people and AI agents respond and act, always with a person's approval.

## What Keiyu does

**Knows.** Connect Google Drive (only the files you choose), WhatsApp Business, email and other systems. Ask anything and get the exact sentence from the source, with a link to the original. When there is no evidence, Keiyu says so. Voice notes are transcribed and citable; the audio is never kept.

**Warns.** A home that shows what is due, what does not add up and what is missing, without being asked. Dates, amounts and currencies are read exactly; what cannot be read is marked as missing, never guessed.

**Answers with you.** A request or a quote arrives by email or WhatsApp. Keiyu shows the full context with its sources and drafts the reply. A person corrects and approves it, it goes out on the same channel, and it is recorded: who wrote it, who approved it and what it was based on.

**Your agents, with rules.** Your company's AI agents get their own credentials, each with a human owner. An agent asks and gets the same grounded answer a person gets; before acting, it asks and is allowed, blocked with a reason, or sent for approval. Every call is recorded.

**Operates across your tools.** A message in Slack becomes a proposed action in Asana or a similar tool, enriched with what Keiyu knows, checked against your rules, executed once and recorded.

## Principles

- If Keiyu cannot show where a figure or a sentence comes from, it does not show it.
- Keiyu says what it does not know and how current each source is.
- Each company's data is isolated. The customer decides how long it is kept and can delete it entirely.
- No model provider trains on customer content.

## Status

Keiyu is in early access and opening its first pilots in Chile. Capabilities are enabled in stages. The product is built in Spanish first, for Chilean formats and the SII.
